Operations Tracker

A quick guide for completing weekly and monthly reports—without GitHub or Cloudflare logins.

You only need your assigned PIN and about 5 minutes.
1
Start here

Choose your role

TravisOffice and systems.
JorgeField operations.
Selecting your name opens the correct task list.
2
Choose the cadence

Weekly or monthly?

Weekly: current execution and follow-up.
Monthly: audits, trends, and compliance.
3
Complete the checklist

Update every task

Check the box when complete.
Choose a status for every task.
Tap + Note for useful details.
Use “Needs attention” plus a note when action is needed.
4
Add the summary

Capture what matters

Wins: what was completed.
Issues: blockers, problems, or decisions needed.
Next priorities: commitments for the next period.
Safety/compliance: incidents, risks, expiring items, or corrective action.
5
Submit

Enter your PIN and send

Confirm the period-ending date, enter your assigned PIN, and tap Submit report.

Wait for “Report # submitted successfully.” If you need to stop, use “Save draft on this device.”
6
Management

Review submitted reports

At the bottom, enter the owner PIN and tap Load submitted reports. Open any report to see task completion, notes, wins, issues, priorities, and safety items.

The owner PIN is only for management.
That’s it

Report clearly.
Follow up quickly.

Use the tracker every reporting period so operations history stays centralized and actionable.
