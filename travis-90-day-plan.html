90-Day Ops Manager Development Plan

90-Day Operations Manager Development Plan — Travis Johnson

Mosquito Joe of Miami | February 2026

Current State

  • Title: Operations Manager
  • Comp: Salary + 0.5% quarterly profit bonus
  • Current responsibilities: Fleet, equipment, inventory/chemical ordering, tech hiring (shared w/ Bev), tech training
  • Strengths: Reliable, techs respect him, hands-on knowledge of every route and service
  • Growth areas: Formal management skills, KPI ownership, delegation, decision-making autonomy

Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-30)

Theme: “Own the Numbers”

Week 1-2: Dashboard & KPI Ownership

    • Jobs/tech/day (target: 10+)
    • Revenue/tech/day (target: $950+)
    • Callback rate (target: <1%)
    • On-time arrival %
    • Chemical usage per route

Week 3-4: Process Documentation

Monthly Check-in (Day 30):

  • Review: Does he understand the KPIs? Is he checking them without being asked?
  • Feedback: What’s going well, what feels overwhelming
  • Adjust: Add or remove responsibilities as needed

Phase 2: Ownership (Days 31-60)

Theme: “Make the Call”

Week 5-6: Decision Authority

    • Travis decides alone: Tech scheduling changes, supply orders <$500, equipment maintenance, route adjustments, customer callbacks
    • Travis recommends, you approve: Tech hiring/firing, equipment purchases >$500, policy changes, customer refunds >$200
    • You/Omar decide: Salary changes, new service offerings, major vendor changes

Week 7-8: Training & Development

    • NPMA QualityPro Manager Training (if QualityPro accredited)
    • Dale Carnegie Leadership Training (Miami sessions)
    • Coursera: “Operations Management” by Wharton (self-paced)

Monthly Check-in (Day 60):

  • Review: Is he making decisions independently? How do the techs respond?
  • Feedback: Review 2-3 decisions he made — what went well, what he’d do differently
  • Metric: Are KPIs improving, stable, or declining under his watch?

Phase 3: Leadership (Days 61-90)

Theme: “Develop Others”

Week 9-10: Build His Team

Week 11-12: Strategic Thinking

90-Day Review:

  • Evaluate:
    • Can he run daily operations without your involvement?
    • Are techs performing at or above prior levels?
    • Has he made at least 3 independent decisions that worked?
    • Does he think about the business or just the tasks?
  • Compensation review: If the 90 days go well, discuss a raise tied to the KPIs he now owns
  • Next 90: Set new goals — maybe P&L literacy, vendor negotiation, or prep for scaling

Quick Wins (Start Immediately)

  1. Give him the tech dashboard link and tell him it’s his scoreboard now
  2. Next time a tech calls you with an ops issue, redirect to Travis
  3. At the next team meeting, publicly reinforce his authority: “Travis is running operations — route questions, equipment, scheduling go to him”
  4. Stop approving his chemical orders — just let him do it

Success Metrics at 90 Days

The goal isn’t to make Travis perfect in 90 days. It’s to shift him from “doing ops tasks” to “managing operations.” The tasks stay the same — the mindset changes.
