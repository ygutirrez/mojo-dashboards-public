Technician Scorecard

Annual-review performance view with transparent, balanced scoring. Click any technician for details.
How the score works: 30% first-time success, 25% jobs per productive workday, 15% revenue per job, 15% review credits, 10% technician-attributed upsells, and 5% driving safety. Peer percentiles use technicians meeting the selected minimum-job threshold. Review credits and safety remain current snapshots because their feeds do not retain dated history. Days out is context only and is not scored.
