Technician Scorecard

Annual-review performance view with transparent, balanced scoring.
How the score works: 30% first-time success, 25% productive workdays, 15% revenue per job, 15% review credits, 10% upsells, and 5% driving safety. Period metrics compare peers with the selected minimum job count. Review credits are cumulative and safety is the latest available snapshot because those feeds do not currently retain dated history. Days out is displayed for review context but is not scored.
